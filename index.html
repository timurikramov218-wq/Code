How to play

  • Press Space, ↑, or tap the page to flip gravity.
  • You can only flip while you're standing on a line.
  • Spikes end your run. So does falling through a hole in the line.
  • Grab yellow stars for +10 points each.
  • It keeps getting faster the longer you last. Every 30 px of paper is 1 point.

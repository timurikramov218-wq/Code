Flying a route

  1. Take off: full throttle, flaps 1, pull back at rotate speed.
  2. Climb out: raise the gear and flaps once you're climbing.
  3. Navigate: follow the amber arrow and the map to your destination.
  4. Approach: line up with the runway, flaps 2, gear down, slow to the landing speed.
  5. Land: touch down gently, then hold Space until you stop. Softer landings earn more stars.
  6. Watch for traffic: other airliners and light planes fly between the airports. They show as diamonds on screen and arrows on the map, and you'll hear their radio calls. Fly into one and it's over.
